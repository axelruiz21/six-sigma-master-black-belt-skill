# CTQ Charter — examples

## Vague ask → charter

**Ask:** “We need to modernize the billing service.”

**Problem statement:** Customers and finance cannot trust invoice amounts within
the agreed billing cycle, causing disputes and manual corrections.

**CTQs:** % invoices needing manual adjust (↓); time-to-correct dispute (↓);
successful automated invoice rate (↑).

**Out of scope:** Rewriting the entire payments platform; UI redesign.

**Next:** `measurement-system` for baselines, then `six-sigma-master-black-belt`
on billing write path.
