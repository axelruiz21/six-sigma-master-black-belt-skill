# Six Sigma MBB Skill Suite

Cursor Agent Skills for **software-systems excellence**: Master Black Belt architecture audits plus adjacent skills for charter, measurement, RCA, value stream, DFSS, FMEA, experiments, control plans, and kaizen.

**Repo:** [axelruiz21/six-sigma-master-black-belt-skill](https://github.com/axelruiz21/six-sigma-master-black-belt-skill)

## What it is

A monorepo of independently installable skills that share one contract: `Y = f(X)`, VOC vs VOP, operational definitions, severity Critical→Low, and a bias toward **deleting unjustified complexity**.

The anchor skill, `six-sigma-master-black-belt`, runs architecture-level **DMAIC** audits. The others feed Define/Measure, deepen Analyze, or execute Improve/Control—without redoing a full architecture review.

## When to reach for it

- Architecture or design review / over-engineering questions
- Project kickoff and CTQ definition
- Postmortems, launch readiness, delivery lead time
- Greenfield design from first principles
- Locking gains after a refactor
